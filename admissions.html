WELCOME TO OUR SCHOOL

THE APPLICATION PROCESS

How to Apply

Use these steps as a guide. The school office will confirm the official application procedure.

1

Contact the Admissions Office

Call or email the school to ask about available places, the appropriate class, application dates, and any application form you need.

2

Confirm Requirements

Ask which documents are required for your child's age and intended class. Requirements may differ by year group and programme.

3

Submit the Application

Complete the school's official application form and submit it with the documents requested by the admissions office.

4

Follow the School's Guidance

The school will advise you about any assessment or placement process, fees, uniform, reporting date, and next steps.

GET PREPARED

Documents to Ask About

The school will confirm the exact documents needed. Depending on the learner's circumstances and year group, families may be asked for items such as:

  • ✓ Completed school application form
  • ✓ Birth certificate or other proof of age
  • ✓ Previous school reports or academic records, where applicable
  • ✓ Transfer or leaving documents, where applicable
  • ✓ Passport-size photographs, if requested
  • ✓ Any additional documents specified by the school

This is a preparation checklist, not a confirmed official document list. Please verify requirements before submitting personal documents.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Admissions FAQs

For the most accurate and current information, please contact the school directly.

When do applications open?

Application dates have not been confirmed on this page. Contact the school office to ask when applications open and when completed forms must be submitted.

What are the school fees?

Please request the current fee structure directly from the school. Confirm any tuition, registration, examination, transport, meals, or other applicable charges with the administration.

Which documents should I bring?

Requirements may vary by class and learner. Ask the admissions office for the official checklist before preparing or submitting documents.

Can I visit the school before applying?

Contact the school to arrange a visit and confirm a suitable day and time before travelling.

How do I know which class is right for my child?

Tell the school the learner's age, current or previous class, and academic background. The admissions team can explain the appropriate placement process.
