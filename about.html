OUR STORY

The Beginning

Joyce Banda Foundation Schools was founded in 1997 by Dr Joyce Banda, former President of the Republic of Malawi. The school is located in Chimwankhunda, Blantyre, Malawi.

From its beginnings, the school has focused on supporting learners through education. Its educational pathways include Reception, Cambridge Primary, and secondary education through MANEB and Cambridge programmes.

Our aim is to provide a supportive learning environment where learners can develop knowledge, confidence, responsibility, and skills for the future.
