OUR APPROACH TO LEARNING

Building Strong Foundations for the Future

Joyce Banda Foundation Schools is located in Chimwankhunda, Blantyre, Malawi. The school's educational pathways include Reception, Cambridge Primary, and secondary education through MANEB and Cambridge programmes.

We believe learning should help children develop academic knowledge, independent thinking, confidence, and the ability to work with others. Families are encouraged to contact the school for current class availability, subject options, and programme details.

LEARNING PATHWAYS

Every Stage Matters

From early learning to secondary education, each stage provides an opportunity to grow. Please contact the school to confirm the classes and programmes currently offered.

✿STAGE 01

Reception

Reception is the beginning of a child's school journey. Early learning helps children develop routines, communication, curiosity, and confidence as they prepare for primary education.

  • Early language and communication
  • Foundational number concepts
  • Social interaction and play
  • Learning routines and independence
▤STAGE 02

Cambridge Primary

The primary years help learners build a broad foundation of knowledge and develop the learning habits needed for further study.

  • Core subject knowledge
  • Reading, writing, and numeracy
  • Problem-solving and enquiry
  • Communication and collaboration
⌑STAGE 03

Secondary Education

Secondary pathways include MANEB and Cambridge programmes. Learners build subject knowledge and prepare for further education and future opportunities.

  • Subject-focused learning
  • Independent study skills
  • Assessment and examination preparation
  • Planning for the next stage

The descriptions above are general overviews, not a verified subject list or official curriculum statement. Ask the school about current classes, subjects, examinations, and programme availability.
