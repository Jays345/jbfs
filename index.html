ABOUT JBF SCHOOLS

Education That Builds Futures

Joyce Banda Foundation Schools was founded in 1997 by Dr. Joyce Banda and is located in Chimwankhunda, Blantyre, Malawi.

We provide education from reception through primary and secondary levels, with Cambridge and MANEB educational pathways.

Our commitment is to help learners develop academically, socially and personally in an environment that encourages curiosity, responsibility and excellence.
